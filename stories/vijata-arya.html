A Life-Changing Connection

Sometimes life-changing connections happen purely by coincidence. My meeting with Dr. Vijata Arya on 18th January 2025 at a wellness event organized for Bollywood junior artists by Dr.365 turned out to be one such remarkable experience. Surrounded by visitors seeking better health solutions, Dr. Vijata stood out with her warmth, deep knowledge, and passionate promotion of Yoga and Naturopathy. Photos and videos from the meeting beautifully capture the energy and positivity of that memorable interaction.

The very next morning, I invited her for a Yoga session at the beach. What began as a casual practice turned into nearly three hours of intense Yoga, meaningful discussions, and inspiring exchanges on holistic living. During breakfast at my home, our conversation shifted towards Ayurveda, natural healing, and wellness retreats. It was then that she encouraged me to experience her renowned naturopathy institute in Gurugram.

"My five-day stay at Kaya Kalp Yoga & Naturopathy Residential Institute became a turning point in my health journey. I completely transformed my perception of food, digestion, and nutrition."

My five-day stay at Kaya Kalp Yoga & Naturopathy Residential Institute in February 2025 became a turning point in my health journey. Under Dr. Vijata’s guidance, I experienced therapeutic fasting for the first time, lost 4 kilograms in just five days, and most importantly, completely transformed my perception of food, digestion, and nutrition.

Meeting her father, Dr. Brijender Arya — revered as the “Bhishma Pitamah of Naturopathy” — further enriched the experience through my insightful discussion for over 30 minutes on Yoga philosophy and wellness science.

This experience with Dr. Vijata Arya offered profound insights into natural healing, disciplined living, and holistic health. Beyond wellness, it evolved into a genuine friendship built on shared values, Yoga, and a common mission to inspire healthier living.
